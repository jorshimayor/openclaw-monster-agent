# Certifications

Four web2 certifications, in the order they should be taken, with prices
verified on 2026-10-13. Re-check before paying — these move.

## The ladder

| # | Certification | Price (exam only) | With course | Validity | Prerequisite |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate** | **$150** | — | 3 years | none |
| 2 | **Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA)** | **$445** | $645 (LFS258) | 2 years | none |
| 3 | **Certified Kubernetes Security Specialist (CKS)** | **$445** | $645 (LFS260) | 2 years | **CKA required** |
| 4 | **OSCP** (OffSec PEN-200) | **$1,749** | included | lifetime | none |

**Exam only: $2,789. With the two Linux Foundation courses: $3,189.**

Notes that change the arithmetic:

- **AWS SAA** — 130 minutes, 65 questions. The cheapest credential here by a
  factor of three, and the broadest hiring signal.
- **CKA and CKS** — each includes one retake and two Killer.sh simulator
  attempts (36 hours each). You get 12 months to schedule. The $200 course
  upgrade is worth it for CKS and skippable for CKA if you already operate
  clusters.
- **CKS cannot be attempted before CKA has been passed.** It is the only
  hard ordering constraint in the table.
- **OSCP** — the $1,749 bundle is 90 days of lab access and **one** exam
  attempt. Learn One at $2,749/year gives two attempts and a full year. The
  bundle is the right call if you can protect the 90 days; the subscription is
  cheaper than failing and rebuying. OffSec also lists up to 20% off Learn One
  for existing certification holders, so take AWS first if you intend to do
  both.

## Order, and why

AWS first because it is $150 and three years of validity for an afternoon's
exam. CKA next because CKS is gated behind it. OSCP last because it is the only
one measured in months rather than days, and because the holder discount
applies once something else is already on the shelf.

## What these are not

None of them help with smart contract auditing. That market reads public
findings — Code4rena, Sherlock, Cantina, CodeHawks placements, a Solodit
profile, Immunefi payouts — and no certificate substitutes. These four are for
the web2 half of the portfolio: infrastructure, orchestration, and offensive
security credibility.

Avoid anything branded "Certified Blockchain Expert". Those cost money and
signal nothing.

## Free, and higher value for the web3 half

Already on the yBorg shelf in `/study`:

- Cyfrin Updraft — security, auditing, formal verification, DeFi. Free.
- Secureum RACE quizzes and Epoch programmes. Free.
- Trail of Bits, building-secure-contracts. Free.
- CodeHawks — free to enter, and you are paid rather than charged.
